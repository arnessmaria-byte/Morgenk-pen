# Vedlegg til videoprompten: gammelt videomanus mot dagens bok

Laget maskinelt 18.10.2026 med `verktoy/lag_videogrunnlag.py`.

- **Gammelt videomanus:** voiceover-linjene i `FORGED_40_Day_Video_Prompts.pdf` (Drive, sist endret 05.08.2026). Det er skrevet mot en eldre versjon av boken.
- **Dagens bok:** `ut/FORGED_manus.md`, endelig tekst 18.10.2026.
- **Merket «ordrett»** betyr at linjen står ord for ord i dagens bok. Det sier ikke at linjen er feil. Mange linjer er korte sammendrag, og de må vurderes etter mening.
- Linjen «The practice for today.» og tittel-linjen er ikke tatt med.

**Sammendrag:** 14 av 280 voiceover-linjer står ordrett i dagens bok. Alle 40 dagtitler stemmer med boken.

## Day 1: Kindling the Flame

**Bibelvers (ESV):** “Do not quench the Spirit.” (1 Thessalonians 5:19)

**Åpningen i boken nå:**

> God never asked you to be flawless. He asked you to be alive.
>
> Most young men who lose their faith never decide to reject it. They lose it the way a man loses a language he never speaks, simply by not using it.
>
> Faith that has never been pushed is still something you take for granted. Faith that has held under pressure becomes a place to stand.

**Voiceover i det gamle videomanuset:**

| Scene | Linje | Ordrett i boken nå? |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Untested faith is an assumption. | nei |
| 4 | Pressured faith becomes a position. | nei |
| 5 | You may call a rope strong. | nei |
| 6 | But weight must go on it. | nei |
| 8 | Name one belief you have never tested. | nei |
| 9 | Ask yourself: Where do I avoid testing it? | nei |
| 10 | Don't fix it. Just observe the flame. | nei |

**The Practice i boken nå:**

> Pick one belief you think is solid but have never tested. It has never made you uncomfortable, met pushback, or cost you anything. Write it down. If it takes a while to find one, the exercise is already working. Then ask:
>
> - What pressure have I let this belief face?
> - Where do I avoid testing it?
> - What would it take to train this belief instead of just assuming it will hold?
>
> Leave the fixing for later. Today you are only striking the match. Just notice where the flame is trying to start.
