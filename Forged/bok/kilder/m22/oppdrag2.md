## Core statements (use them as written or very close, so the theology reads the same everywhere)

- **CREED (Introduction):** "This book stands on Scripture, the only rule that is never itself put on trial: "Scripture cannot be broken" (John 10:35). - **ORDER (Introduction, Day 4):** "In God there is order without lesser worth. The Father sends, the Son is sent, and the Spirit proceeds from the Father, yet the three are one God, equal in glory. Paul ties the order of creation to that order: "the head of every man is Christ, the head of a wife is her husband, and the head of Christ is God" (1 Corinthians 11:3). Order does not mean lesser worth. That is the lie a demasculinized culture tells you, and many churches now repeat it."

Keep it to two to four sentences.
- **JEZEBEL (Day 12 or Day 35, and one sentence in the Introduction):** "Scripture names this pattern. Christ rebuked the church in Thyatira for tolerating "that woman Jezebel, who calls herself a prophetess" (Revelation 2:20). The spirit of Jezebel is never content with tolerance. It takes the pulpit and sets aside God's order of creation (1 Timothy 2:12-13).
