## Day 26: The Morning You Don't Feel Like Facing

> “O LORD, in the morning you hear my voice.”
>
> Psalm 5:3

Some mornings fight you before the day starts. Your body is slow, your willpower is low, and your purpose feels far away. What you do then shows whether your training holds up with nothing carrying you.

### The Scenario

The alarm goes off at 6:15. No test is waiting, and your shift at the bike shop does not start until noon. You just feel heavy. You hit snooze twice, then lie on your back scrolling while the gym bag you packed last night sits by the door. Your Bible stays shut on the nightstand. At 7:40 you get up, already behind, and you spend the rest of the day catching up.

### Avoiding the Day vs. Showing Up

| Avoiding the Day | Showing Up |
| :- | :- |
| Hits snooze | Gets up at the alarm |
| Waits for motivation | Acts without it |
| Scrolls first | Prays first |
| Makes excuses | Makes the bed |
| Drags the whole day down | Sets the day's rhythm |

The day asks you to show up. Excitement is optional.

### What This Day Is Really About

You know the argument you have with yourself each morning under the blanket. Answer it with the first act of the day, before you feel ready.

Discipline follows a direction you have already chosen. It does not wait for your feelings to agree. In Christ you can pray David's words, "in the morning you hear my voice," and mean them on the heavy mornings too.

### The Practice

Look back at your last few mornings. Ask:

- What does my hand reach for when the alarm goes off?
- What do I tell myself to earn ten more minutes?

**Today's Action:** Before 10 tonight, set one alarm. On a card, write the one sentence you will pray out loud when it rings, feet on the floor, before any screen. Lay the card on your phone.

### Healing Forge: When Mornings Feel Heavy

Mornings may once have filled you with dread: conflict, demands, responsibility with no one to help you carry it. Avoiding them protected you. You can come back to the morning one step at a time, and you do not have to be perfect. What counts is that you choose to get up. Through Christ, the Father hears your first tired sentence. If the heaviness will not let up, talk to a pastor, a doctor, or a licensed counselor.

*End of Day 26*
