## Day 20: Strength Grows in Brotherhood

> “Iron sharpens iron, and one man sharpens another.”
>
> Proverbs 27:17

Strength gets steadier when you stay close to other men. Your beliefs get clearer when honest men test them.

### The Scenario

Levi lifted alone at 5 a.m. with his earbuds in. He read his Bible alone at the kitchen table. On Sundays he sat in the back row and left during the last song. No one called out his excuses. His bench press had not moved in eight months, and his prayers had gone stale. What he lacked was friction: other men pushing back.

### Isolation vs. Brotherhood

| Isolation | Brotherhood |
| :- | :- |
| Avoids being seen | Asks to be checked on |
| Stays comfortable | Accepts tension |
| Guards your independence | Lets others sharpen you |
| Shuts out correction | Takes correction |
| Gets stuck | Gets stronger |

Other men can show you blind spots you will miss alone.

### What This Day Is Really About

The number of your friends matters less than their quality. Brotherhood means heading the same way and letting each other say hard things. Without friction, what you believe goes dull.

Jesus chose twelve men to be with Him, and His church is still where "one man sharpens another." Paul told Timothy to pass on what he had heard: "entrust to faithful men, who will be able to teach others also" (2 Timothy 2:2). Look for those men, tested over years and standing on the apostles' teaching.

You need them because a man alone drifts with the age, and many pulpits already have. Brothers who stand on the word will hold you to it. When they correct you, repent: the pull toward approval that bent those pulpits works on you too.

### The Practice

Think about the men around you. Ask:

- Who sees my habits clearly?
- Who can challenge me without me brushing him off?
- Which of them obeys Scripture when it costs him?

**Today's Action:** By 10 p.m., text one brother who holds to Scripture. Ask to meet this week for breakfast or a workout, and name one habit you want him to check on.

### Healing Forge: When Brotherhood Was Missing

Other men may have been unsafe for you: they competed with you, mocked you, or betrayed you. Being alone felt safer. But staying alone to avoid risk also means avoiding growth. Healing does not ask for instant trust. Let one carefully chosen man see you, someone who can sharpen you without threatening you.
