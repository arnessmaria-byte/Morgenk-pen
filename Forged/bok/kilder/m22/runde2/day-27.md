## Day 27: Staying Awake on Purpose

> “So then let us not sleep, as others do, but let us keep awake and be sober.”
>
> 1 Thessalonians 5:6

Drift makes no noise. It happens slowly, through small things you allow without noticing. Most men never decide to turn away from where they are headed. They fall asleep on the way.

### The Scenario

Your overnight security shift at a distribution center starts at midnight. Every hour you walk the fence line. On the first two rounds you rattle every door handle. By the fourth you stay in the warm booth, eyes closing between glances at the monitors. At 4:40 a camera shows the gate by the loading dock standing open. You have no idea how long it has been that way.

### Apostolic Church vs. Feminized Church

| Apostolic Church | Feminized Church |
| :- | :- |
| Preaches repentance | Says all is well |
| Submits to Scripture | Bends Scripture to culture |
| Ordains tested men | Ordains women as pastors |

Test a church by what it does, whatever it calls itself.

### What This Day Is Really About

A sentry seems to do nothing at all, until the one night his watching really counts. You can keep believing and still leave the gate open.

God warned, "Do not listen to the words of the prophets who prophesy to you, filling you with vain hopes" (Jeremiah 23:16). Peter warned that false teachers would rise inside the church (2 Peter 2:1). They offer peace without repentance, put women in the pulpit, and bless sin. Women and men bear God's image equally (Genesis 1:27). The office Christ gave is a different question. Paul told Christians waiting for Jesus to "keep awake and be sober." Repent first of the easy lies you have swallowed.

### The Practice

- What have I let slide that once bothered me?
- Whose teaching have I stopped testing against Scripture?

**Today's Action:** By 10 p.m., list the three voices you hear most on faith. Unfollow any that offers peace without repentance, and write one line on why.

### Healing Forge: When Staying Alert Wears You Out

If you learned to scan for danger and never rest, because you could never tell what was coming, that restlessness was how you survived. The alertness asked of you here is calm and rests on trust in the God who never sleeps (Psalm 121:4). What you are guarding is your direction. You are not asked to brace for everything to fall apart.
