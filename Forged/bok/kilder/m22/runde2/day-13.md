## Day 13: When Attachment Pulls You Off Course

> “Whoever loves father or mother more than me is not worthy of me.”
>
> Matthew 10:37

Attachment can be a good thing. But when it takes the wrong place in your life, it bends your choices before you notice. Whatever you are loyal to will shape where you drift.

### The Scenario

At twenty-three, Isaac was the youngest man on his church council. One Tuesday night the council voted on whether to start blessing same-sex unions. His father, a member for thirty years, said the church had to keep its young families. Every other hand went up. The chair asked Isaac to make it unanimous, for the sake of unity. Isaac loved his father and that church. He kept his hand on the table and voted no. His father said nothing on the drive home.

### Misplaced Attachment vs. Christ First

| Misplaced Attachment | Christ First |
| :- | :- |
| Avoids loss | Accepts the cost |
| Keeps relationships intact | Keeps your direction |
| Puts off obeying | Obeys when God asks |
| Buys peace at any price | Stays the same everywhere |
| Feels kind | Stays faithful |

Attachment goes wrong when it makes God's decisions.

### What This Day Is Really About

Put your loyalties in the right order. That does not mean cutting people off. If this day tempts you to drop someone who is just inconvenient, you have misread it. Jesus claims first place, ahead of "father or mother" and every other love, including your love for a church. The church belongs to Christ, and no council vote outranks His word. When a council votes against what He said, "from the beginning of creation, 'God made them male and female'" (Mark 10:6), stand with Christ, even if you stand alone. If you have kept silent just to keep your place, confess it and speak up.

### The Practice

Name the person or group you most fear letting down.

- What have I put off so I would not lose them?
- Which loyalty is trying to outrank Christ?

**Today's Action:** Tonight, before 10, write down one step of obedience you have put off to keep someone happy. Write beside it the date you will take it, and tell one believer what you wrote.

### Healing Forge: When Pleasing People Protected You

Pleasing people may once have kept you safe from an angry parent or a shaky home. It made sense then. But it can grow into the fear of man, which Scripture calls a snare (Proverbs 29:25). If that fear has kept you silent about Christ, confess it; He forgives and cleanses (1 John 1:9). Putting Him first betrays no one.
