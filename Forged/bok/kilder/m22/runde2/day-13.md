## Day 13: When Attachment Pulls You Off Course

> “Whoever loves father or mother more than me is not worthy of me.”
>
> Matthew 10:37

Attachment can be a good thing. But when it takes the wrong place in your life, it bends your choices before you notice. Whatever you are loyal to will shape where you drift.

### The Scenario

Isaac became a Christian at nineteen, and a year later his family still did not know. His dad joked about "church people" at dinner. His girlfriend said faith was fine if he did not get weird about it. Isaac did not feel torn. He felt responsible for everyone, so he waited for a better time. He never dropped his faith. He just hid it so he would not lose anyone.

### Misplaced Attachment vs. Christ First

| Misplaced Attachment | Christ First |
| :- | :- |
| Avoids loss | Accepts the cost |
| Keeps relationships intact | Keeps your direction |
| Puts off obeying | Obeys when God asks |
| Buys peace at any price | Stays the same everywhere |
| Feels kind | Stays faithful |

Attachment goes wrong when it makes decisions that belong to God.

### What This Day Is Really About

Put your loyalties in the right order. That does not mean cutting people off. If this day tempts you to drop someone who is just inconvenient, you have misread it. Love goes wrong when it takes the place of obedience. Some attachments whisper: *Stay here. Don't change. Don't take risks.* When they outrank what God asks, you lose your direction slowly, without ever openly rebelling. Jesus claims first place, ahead of "father or mother" and every other love.

### The Practice

Name one attachment that affects whether you obey.

- Who am I trying not to disappoint?
- What have I put off so I would not lose someone?
- Which loyalty is trying to have the final say?

**Today's Action:** Before bed, write down one step you have put off to keep someone happy. Next to it, write the day you will take it and the name of one believer you will talk to first.

### Healing Forge: When Pleasing People Protected You

Pleasing others may have kept you safe from harm, from a life that felt shaky, or from being abandoned. That was wise once. But what helped you survive is rarely meant to guide your whole life. Reordering your loyalties is part of growing up, and it betrays no one. What once protected you may now be holding you back.

*End of Day 13*
