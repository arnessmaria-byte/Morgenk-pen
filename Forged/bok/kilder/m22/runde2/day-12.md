## Day 12: When Standing Out Costs You

> “Woe to you, when all people speak well of you.”
>
> Luke 6:26

Standing out is rarely punished right away. The cost comes slowly: people grow colder, and fewer doors open.

### The Scenario

Queen Jezebel had killed the Lord's prophets. On Mount Carmel, Elijah came near to all the people and said, "How long will you go limping between two different opinions?" (1 Kings 18:21). If the Lord was God, he told them, they should follow him. The people did not answer him a word. Elijah said he alone was left as a prophet of the Lord, against 450 prophets of Baal.

### Seeking Approval vs. Accepting Cost

| Seeking Approval | Accepting Cost |
| :- | :- |
| Puts comfort first | Puts truth first |
| Bends your beliefs | Puts up with friction |
| Avoids being noticed | Takes the consequences |
| Keeps doors open | Keeps your integrity |
| Feels safe | Stands firm |

You can stand out without picking fights, but never for free.

### What This Day Is Really About

If you have watered down what you believe to stay liked, repent. Jesus warned, "Woe to you, when all people speak well of you," because false prophets got the same applause. Jezebel's pattern did not die with her. Christ rebuked the church in Thyatira for tolerating "that woman Jezebel, who calls herself a prophetess" (Revelation 2:20). The spirit of Jezebel is never content with tolerance. It takes the pulpit against God's order of creation (1 Timothy 2:12-13), ordains women as pastors and bishops against the apostolic rule that overseers be tested men (1 Timothy 3:2; Titus 1:6), blesses same-sex unions against Christ's own words (Mark 10:6-9), and pushes out pastors who will not go along. Women and men bear God's image equally (Genesis 1:27). The office Christ gave is a different question.

### The Practice

Elijah's question is for you before it is for any church.

- Where do I limp between two opinions to keep my seat?
- What small step would cost me some comfort today?

**Today's Action:** By 10 tonight, turn down one plan or say the honest sentence you keep swallowing. Take that small step without drama or announcement. Most costs are paid in private.

### Healing Forge: When Rejection Reopens Old Wounds

People pulling away can bring back old pain from rejection: bullying, being left out, being overlooked. Do not read today through that old wound: distance does not always mean you have been abandoned. After Carmel, Elijah ran and asked to die, and God fed him and let him sleep (1 Kings 19:3-8).
