## Day 30: When God Feels Distant

> “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?”
>
> Psalm 22:1

Feeling distant is not the same as being abandoned. Sometimes distance means silence where there used to be noise, and stillness where comfort used to come quickly. Many men think this means they are losing their faith. Often it means something else.

### The Scenario

Micah still set his alarm for 6 a.m. to pray before his warehouse shift. He read his chapter and was in church every Sunday. But God felt far away, and his prayers met silence. He searched his week for hidden sin. He tried kneeling, praying out loud, praying at night. Nothing changed. The silence bothered him less than the fear that it meant something was wrong.

### Assuming God Is Gone vs. Holding On

| Assuming God Is Gone | Holding On |
| :- | :- |
| Takes silence as rejection | Knows God is still there |
| Wants comfort right away | Keeps going without answers |
| Relies on feelings | Lives by what he believes |
| Sees silence as failure | Sees growth in silence |
| Pulls away | Keeps obeying |

Often, silence means reassurance is being held back.

### What This Day Is Really About

Today is about staying faithful without that reassurance, whether or not the feelings return. Sooner or later, your growth stops running on excitement and starts running on commitment. Early on, God often feels close because your faith is still being built. Later, your faith holds because you have chosen it.

God does not always speak to ease your discomfort. He knows what is in you, and now He is letting you see what has been built there. Jesus cried "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" from the cross, so you can pray those words without shame.

### The Practice

Do not chase anything new today: no new message, no signs, no fresh reassurance. Do what you already know is right. Keep showing up, and stay faithful.

- What have I dropped since God went quiet?
- Where am I waiting for a feeling before I obey?

**Today's Action:** Tonight, pray out loud for five minutes and tell God plainly that He feels far away. Then do one right thing you have put off, like calling the friend you owe an apology.

### Healing Forge: When Silence Feels Like Abandonment

Silence may once have meant danger for you: people cutting you off, pulling away, leaving you alone with no explanation. When God is silent, those memories can wake up. His silence is not neglect. He has not let go of you.
