## Day 34: A Credible Witness

> “You will be my witnesses…”
>
> Acts 1:8

Being a witness means being the same man at school, at work, at home, and online. It is not a show. A life that matches your words convinces more than any argument, and over time people find it hard to ignore.

### The Scenario

Logan never argued about religion or posted verses online. At the grocery store where he stocked shelves, he was patient with rude customers whether the manager was watching or not. When the guys in the back swapped crude weekend stories, he kept working and changed the subject. One night after close, a coworker asked why he never seemed rattled. Logan told him about Jesus in a few plain sentences. The coworker listened, because he had watched Logan for a year.

### Show vs. Witness

| Show | Witness |
| :- | :- |
| Tries to convince | Lets people wonder |
| Says more than he lives | Speaks from how he lives |
| Wants a reaction | Gives people time |
| Performs his beliefs | Lives them |
| Needs attention | Earns trust |

People see your witness more clearly over time.

### What This Day Is Really About

Living your faith every day comes first, and it makes your words worth hearing. The strongest witness is a life where your actions match what you believe. People notice, and their trust in you grows on its own.

You cannot make a witness happen. You can protect what makes it possible: being the same man everywhere. Jesus told His followers, "You will be my witnesses," which means pointing to Him with your life and, when the moment comes, with your words.

### The Practice

Think about school, work, home, and your phone. Ask:

- Where do I act differently than I do on Sunday?
- What do I do online that no one at church sees?

**Today's Action:** Pick one place where your actions and beliefs do not match, and fix it before bed. Apologize to your sister for how you spoke to her, or delete the account you keep hidden. Do not make a show of it.

### Healing Forge: When Being Noticed Once Felt Unsafe

Being seen may once have meant risk: being picked apart, misunderstood, or punished for being honest. So you learned to stay hidden. Being a witness asks you to be steady, not to put yourself on display. Be faithful where you already stand, and do not worry about who notices.
