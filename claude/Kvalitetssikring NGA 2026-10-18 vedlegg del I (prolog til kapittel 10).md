## Prologue, "The Borrowed Drum"

## Summary

Counts: **BLOCKER 1; MAJOR 12** (C1, C3, prologue x2, ch4 x1, ch5 x3, ch6 x1, ch7 x1, ch10 x1); **MINOR 27**. Clean across all twelve texts: em dashes, American spelling, slang, Norse material, commercial pointers, chapter endings (H.4/H.5), population transfer (H.9a), and the D.3 "family failed / strong family protects" mechanisms.

The three issues that matter most in this part:

1. **Chapter 7 crosses H.15 by implication (BLOCKER).** "I will not give you a person to blame" (l.9), "partly because it concerns me" (l.7) and "A father kept at a distance" (l.21) together tell the reader the author did not choose the absence and that someone else is responsible. Under H.15.6 that makes legal review mandatory unless the sentences are removed; removing them costs the chapter nothing, because the rest of it is general and well judged. Reinstate the third approved sentence in the same place.
2. **The prologue describes the author with a figure and prays for the son's "protection" (two MAJORs).** "I had the figure of Neo from The Matrix in mind" breaks H.3/I.13.3 on the first page; "I asked him to intervene and protect my son" implies a danger and a party. Both fixes are one-line.
