# Vedlegg del II: chapters 11-20 ("The Country That Still Works") against Book Constitution v2.3

Uavhengig dellesning, 18. oktober 2026. Linjenummer viser til kapittelfilene (ett avsnitt per linje). Språk: engelsk, som levert.

General notes that apply to the whole Part (stated once, not repeated per chapter):

- **No em dashes anywhere in chapters 11-20 or their notes.** Only en dashes in ranges and in "parent–child" (ch 19 line 15). I.14 passes on this point.
- **No American spellings found** (judgement, sceptical, towards, per cent, practised, organisation are all British). One inconsistency: "learned" (ch 20 line 3) against "learnt" (ch 17 line 19). MINOR, I.14.
- **No youth slang, no dates or timetables, no dread or contempt endings, no nostalgia-as-history** in any of the ten chapters. H.4, H.5 and I.10 "nostalgia" pass for every chapter.
- **Endnote numbering**: every superscript in the body of chapters 11-19 matches an existing numbered entry and no entry is orphaned (ch 13 and 14 reuse a note number twice, which is fine).
- **Arithmetic**: every difference and percentage that could be recomputed from the notes is correct (94,901 and 2.7 per cent; 1,263 and 236 and 23 and 701; 51.3/26.1/77.4; 53.4/37.9/8.8; 438; 0.9/9.0/4.1; 22+52+26 = 100; 70.8+19.5+9.7 = 100.0; 67.4+20.2+12.3 = 99.9). Body and notes agree on every figure printed in both.
- **Norwegian statistic class label (I.11.10 / H.14)**: none of the ten chapters uses the five class words (membership, participation, household form, relationship quality, outcome) as labels. Ch 13 says "registered members"; ch 15 says "living arrangement"; ch 14, 16, 17 describe the measure in prose. The substance is usually there but the labelling rule is not applied as written. MINOR Part-wide; recommend one clause per figure.
- **Part-level structural point (MAJOR, I.15 / I.7 / I.17.1 / I.17.8)**: a near-identical paragraph about "the Jewish history of the Solbak Årnes family" recurs in every chapter (ch 11 l.3 and l.21; 12 l.33; 13 l.3 and l.23; 14 l.13 and l.33; 15 l.25; 16 l.27; 17 l.19 and l.25; 18 passim; 19 l.33; 20 l.9). The handoff records this as an authorial ruling later than the Constitution, so it is not a BLOCKER, but (a) the Constitution's reader profile (I.7: an Anglophone son, not the author's family), central question (I.2) and exclusion rules (I.15: "a book about the author's exceptional identity") have not been amended to admit it; (b) the paragraphs read as boilerplate inserted after drafting, often with no connection to the chapter's subject (worst case ch 12 l.33, after a paragraph on satisfaction with administrative services). Either amend the Constitution or cut the thread to the two or three chapters where it does work.
- **Part-level (MAJOR, I.17.5)**: chapters 11-19 contain no Christological content (Christ is named only inside the Church's own rite descriptions in ch 14, and in ch 20). By I.17.5 each of these chapters "is not yet Christian". This may be intended for a descriptive Part, but the Constitution says "every chapter"; an explicit editorial ruling is needed rather than silent exemption.

## Summary

**Counts**: BLOCKER 2; MAJOR 11 (including 2 Part-level); MINOR approx. 45 (style and labelling, listed above).

**The two blockers (both H.7 mandatory counterfindings missing from Part II and, on grep, from the entire manuscript)**:
1. "births, fertility ... rose in 2025 compared with 2024": baptism and confirmation are printed (ch 14) but births and fertility are not compared with 2024 anywhere (ch 15 gives only the 2025 count). Needs source packet.
2. "youth loneliness fell from its pandemic peak, while overall adult loneliness remained above its 2020 baseline": absent from every chapter file (the only hit is the word "lonely" in ch 30). Needs source packet and a home, most naturally ch 12 or ch 19.

**Three most important issues in this Part**:
1. The two missing H.7 counterfindings above, plus the partial one in ch 16 (2000-2010 printed where the Constitution requires 1970-2010; MAJOR).
2. Ch 13 l.13 "In the same Statistics Norway membership series": the two totals are sourced to two different SSB tables in notes 1 and 4, the handoff confirms two published aggregations exist, and the 2024 to 2025 movement is not printed (I.11.9, I.11.11). Needs source packet.
3. Ch 19 l.13: Dohmen et al. printed without its population (Germany), the one place in Part II where the H.9a population rule is broken; and ch 16 l.3, the Part's headline father-specific figure, which carries neither source nor country in the passage (L3c/H.6).

Two decisions are needed above the chapter level rather than by redraft: whether the Constitution is amended to admit the Solbak Årnes Jewish-history thread that now runs through every chapter (I.7, I.15), and whether descriptive chapters 11-19 are exempt from I.17 test 5.
